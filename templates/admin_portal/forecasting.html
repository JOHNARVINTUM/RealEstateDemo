These forecasts use a 3-month moving average — a simple, reliable method that smooths short-term fluctuations and projects the near-term trend forward. Accuracy metrics are computed by backtesting on the last 3 months of real data.

RMSE

Root Mean Square Error. Penalizes large errors more heavily — lower is better.

MAE

Mean Absolute Error. Average prediction error in the same unit as the data — lower is better.

MAPE

Mean Absolute Percentage Error. Under 5% is good, under 15% is acceptable, and anything higher is high error.

Future versions will compare this against SARIMA for seasonal modeling.
