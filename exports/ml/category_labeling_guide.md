# Maintenance Category Labeling Guide

Permitted labels:

## PLUMBING
- leaks, water flow, faucet, sink, toilet, drain, pipes, shower, bidet, drainage, hose, and related concerns

## ELECTRICAL
- power, outlets, sockets, wiring, breakers, lights, bulbs, switches, sparks, short circuits, and related concerns

## STRUCTURAL
- walls, ceilings, floors, cracks, doors, windows, roofs, tiles, stairs, gates, locks, cabinets, and related concerns

## OTHER
- requests that do not reasonably belong to Plumbing, Electrical, or Structural

Labeling rules:

1. Review the combined meaning of the title and description.
2. Assign the category representing the primary maintenance concern.
3. For a request containing multiple concerns, assign the category of the main repair being requested.
4. If no single primary issue can reasonably be identified, assign OTHER.
5. Do not inspect the detector?s keywords or system prediction while labeling.
6. Use only the exact uppercase labels PLUMBING, ELECTRICAL, STRUCTURAL, or OTHER.
7. Do not leave labels blank.
8. Do not modify the maintenance title or description.
