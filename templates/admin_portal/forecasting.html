Forecast Model Basis

How the SARIMA decision-support forecast is built and compared against the other models.

Source history comes from actual collected revenue in MonthlyBill using rent, water, parking, and paid-interest totals grouped by month. The system computes Naive, ARIMA, and SARIMA results from the same monthly collection history, but the dashboard always presents SARIMA as the active forecast while the other two remain comparison baselines.

AI Integration Flow

What happens from billing history to dashboard output.

1. Historical Revenue

Collected MonthlyBill amounts are grouped by month to create the time-series input.

2. Model Comparison

Naive, ARIMA, and SARIMA are backtested on the latest completed months and scored using RMSE, MAE, and MAPE.

3. Forecast Output

SARIMA returns the next three monthly forecasts plus a confidence band when available.

4. Admin Decision Support

Forecast values support planning and monitoring only. They do not override actual billing, payment, or administrative judgment.
